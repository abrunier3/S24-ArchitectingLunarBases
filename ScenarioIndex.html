          Energy-Unconstrained Mode: no active power-generation asset was selected in Step 2, so DES treats energy as unlimited.
        


        

          
1. TRANSPORT

          
Operating coefficients controlling rover cycles, ISRU throughput, and LOX dispatch.

        


        

          

            Energy coefficient [Both rover types]
            0.00034
          

          
          
Energy consumed per kilometer and kilogram transported: energy = coefficient × distance × payload.

        


        

          

            Flat-terrain rover speed [Both rover types]
            0.20 km/h
          

          
          
Nominal rover speed on flat ground. The DES reduces it from the measured route slope.

        


        

          

            Slope speed reduction [Both rover types]
            5% / deg
          

          
          
Terrain penalty: effective speed = flat speed / (1 + penalty × route slope in degrees).

        


        

          
Rover payload by transported resource

          

        


        

          
2. ISRU PLANT CONFIGURATION

          
Parameters used only by the ISRU scenario engine.

        


        

          

            Processing rate [ISRUPlant]
            1600 kg/hr
          

          
          
Regolith mass processed per plant per hour; determines the duration of each processing batch.

        


        

        

          

            Pickup threshold [ISRUPlant → LOXRover]
            100 kg
          

          
          
Minimum LOX stored at one plant before it requests a rover from the shared LOX rover pool.
